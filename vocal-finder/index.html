Vocal Finder

Find free vocal samples in a key, then listen to them one after another.

Samples come from Freesound, which needs a free key. Get one here (sign in, fill in the short form, then copy the “Client secret/Api key”). It's saved in this browser only.

Shortcuts: Space play/pause · ↓ or J next · ↑ or K previous

    How the key is worked out

    Each sample is matched in one of two ways, shown on its row:

    • Labelled: the creator wrote the key in the name or tags (for example “Vocal_Am_120bpm”). Usually reliable.
    • Detected: Freesound analysed the audio and estimated the key. Good for sung notes, less so for speech or noisy recordings.

    Always trust your ears: a quick listen tells you if it sits in your track.
